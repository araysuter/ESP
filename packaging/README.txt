ESP - Earth-scale rocket sandbox

macOS: open the DMG and drag ESP to Applications. The app is not Apple-notarized.
Windows: run the Setup.exe installer, or extract the entire portable ZIP and run
ESP.exe. Keep ESP_Data, UnityPlayer.dll and all other files beside the executable.
The Windows installer is not Authenticode signed.

Choose a launch site and vehicle, then launch. M switches map/flight views;
Escape opens the menu. The in-game help lists the remaining controls.
Offline Earth imagery, celestial sky and standard atmosphere are bundled.
Live imagery/weather require an internet connection and have offline fallbacks.

Licenses, model credits, data sources and limitations are in Notices/.
The exact source revision and version are in build-info.json.

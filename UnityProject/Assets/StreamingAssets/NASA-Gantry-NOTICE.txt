ESP adaptations: uniform normalization to approximately 105.7 m total height,
centered horizontal bounds, ground-base pivot, three original material groups,
triangulated Y-up OBJ, compression and Unity URP materials. No decimation or
replacement geometry. Historical shuttle-era scenery placed illustratively
beside the mission pad; not a surveyed or current LC-39A reconstruction.

# Launch weather profile

`LaunchWeatherProfile` retrieves Open-Meteo hourly model output for the requested location, using UTC and the latest returned hour at or before fetch time. It requests pressure-level temperature, wind speed/direction and geopotential height at 1000, 925, 850, 700, 500, 300, 200, 100 and 50 hPa. Surface pressure, 2 m temperature and 10 m wind from that same hour form an approximate surface sample at the API's returned model-grid elevation. These heights differ; this is a practical near-surface approximation, not a measured vertical profile. Pressure is surface pressure, not sea-level pressure. Below-ground pressure levels are excluded.

Samples use meters above mean sea level (geopotential height approximated as geometric MSL altitude), kelvin, pascals and east/north meters per second. Meteorological wind direction is the direction **from** which wind blows. Missing pressure levels are omitted; interpolation is linear between valid samples and never extrapolates. A caller must explicitly use its deterministic standard atmosphere outside the available range or when no profile is available. Abrupt transitions at the boundary require review before flight integration. No flight forces are applied here.

These are gridded modeled forecasts or analyses, not launch-site observations; spatial and temporal resolution, terrain, gusts, vertical winds, and uncertainty limit their use. A single snapshot is fixed in time, not a forecast along the rocket trajectory. Data: Open-Meteo, CC BY 4.0; converted to SI and interpolated. See mission-integration.md for source and service terms. Source: https://open-meteo.com/en/docs (pressure levels and geopotential height). Flight integration, aerodynamic response, fallback validation and trajectory testing remain separate work.

A new request clears the previous snapshot before validation or network access, so a failed site change cannot reuse the old site's data. The selected hour must be no more than two hours old at fetch; consumers should also assess age during a long session. `VerifyLaunchWeatherProfile.Run` is a local synthetic editor verification entry point; it does not contact the API. Newtonsoft JSON 3.2.2 is pinned directly in the manifest. The synthetic verification passed in Unity 6000.6.9f1 on the macOS VM. MissionDirector checks snapshot age again at launch and passes null for an expired profile.
